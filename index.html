Start here

Connect directly to an ELM327 OBD-II adapter in your car, or launch the simulator to practice.

  1. 1
    Plug in your ELM327 adapter

    Plug your ELM327 adapter firmly into the OBD-II port under the dash.

  2. 2
    Turn key to ON (KOEO), choose target & connect

    Turn the key to ON (Key On, Engine Off — KOEO) to power the ECU, then connect your adapter and arm Cold start / creep capture or choose your Scan target before cranking.

  3. 3
    Start the engine & mark symptoms

    Start the engine (for a cold start, start immediately after connecting so t=0 records cranking and initial idle) and tap Mark moment whenever you feel a symptom.

Classic Bluetooth pairing: Pair the adapter in your phone or computer's Bluetooth settings first (PIN is usually 1234 or 0000), then tap Connect: Classic / USB.

Browser & adapter limits: Requires HTTPS and Chrome or Edge (desktop or Android). WiFi ELM327 adapters are unsupported — browsers cannot open raw TCP sockets to 192.168.0.10. Never operate the screen while moving.

Capture mode: logging RPM + Speed + RPM/speed. Tap Mark shudder / symptom when you feel it.

    What is verified vs. what is experimental
    
            
    
              BayScope's standard SAE J1979 Mode 01 PID path, supported-PID bitmask scan (0100, 0120, 0140), Mode 09 VIN reader, Mode 01 01 readiness & MIL check, and Mode 03 / 07 / 0A trouble-code reader are implemented directly against the ELM327 protocol and mirrored in the built-in simulator.
            
    
            
    
              Manufacturer-specific PIDs are not bundled as verified databases. The two seeded transmission temperature PIDs (Jatco CVT on 7E1 and Toyota ATF on 7E0) are marked unverified — confirm on your car because automakers move Mode 21/22 addresses across model years and ECUs. If a custom PID reads nonsense (such as a stuck -40 or 215), delete or adjust it in Custom PIDs.
            
    
            
    
              OBD-II data points toward causes — it does not prove them. A lean code (P0171) and high fuel trims narrow the search to unmetered air, MAF error, or fuel delivery, but cannot replace physical inspection, smoke testing, fuel-pressure gauges, or compression checks.
            
    

            
    Adapter & browser requirements
    
            
      
              
    • HTTPS + Chrome or Edge on desktop or Android is required for Web Bluetooth (BLE) and Web Serial (Classic Bluetooth SPP / USB).
      • 
              
    • Classic Bluetooth adapters (~$20 AutoZone / ELM327 clones): Pair the adapter in your phone or OS Bluetooth settings first (PIN is usually 1234 or 0000), then use Connect: Classic / USB.
      • 
              
    • WiFi ELM327 adapters are unsupported because browsers cannot open raw TCP sockets to adapter IPs like 192.168.0.10:35000.
      • 
              
    • Safety: Always test parked first. Have a passenger watch the screen or use Mark moment only when stopped safely.
